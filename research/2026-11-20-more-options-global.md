# Bangladesh and Indonesia storefronts and additional Falkenstein configurations

Public pages, product lookups and anonymous price calculators were checked on 20 November 2026 in Vietnam (19 November UTC). The regular Hetzner AX162-1 reference is €612.30/month plus €304 setup. The [ECB's dated EUR/USD series](https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.USD.EUR.SP00.A?startPeriod=2026-11-19&endPeriod=2026-11-19&format=csvdata) and [EUR/IDR series](https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.IDR.EUR.SP00.A?startPeriod=2026-11-19&endPeriod=2026-11-19&format=csvdata) give USD 1.0992 and IDR 20,073.1 per EUR. No account was created, domain supplied, order placed, stock reserved or vendor contacted.

## Hostnin

The Bangladeshi provider's [bare-metal server catalog](https://hostnin.com/hosting/dedicated-server) lists the EPYC RAM and EPYC Storage models as exclusive hardware with full root access. Both list an AMD EPYC 9454P with 48 physical cores, **two 3.84 TB Gen4 NVMe SSDs**, a 1 Gbit/s port and unmetered traffic. EPYC RAM has 256 GB installed and EPYC Storage has 128 GB. The card's compact storage badge says 7.50 TB while the detailed line and both order links specify 2 × 3.84 TB (7.68 TB nominal raw). Drive capacity exceeds the floor on either description. RAM generation, installed RAID, physical location and IPv4 allotment are unspecified. A mirror of these drives would have 3.84 TB nominal capacity.

The [EPYC RAM product lookup](https://hostnin.com/api/products/dedicated?slug=epyc-ram) identifies PID 151 and [EPYC Storage](https://hostnin.com/api/products/dedicated?slug=epyc-storage) identifies PID 152. Both returned HTTP 200 with success and the correct product name. The lookup returns null for pricing. Prices come from the live catalog and the site's [public order calculator](https://hostnin.com/_next/static/chunks/1w9fjdb4o_ao2.js?dpl=1c575453a0d9f299a961fe5b2c5b9f7aa50bebac), which resolves the product ID by slug and divides the order-link's BDT amounts by 120 when USD is selected. This is the seller's USD billing tariff. The [international terms](https://hostnin.com/terms-and-conditions) specify that international customers are charged USD and the [USD payment method lookup](https://hostnin.com/api/payment-methods?currency=USD) returned Stripe credit/debit cards for USD. Dedicated hardware is excluded from the routine 30-day refund per the catalog FAQ.

## Hostiware's $270 headline conflicts with its billing catalog

The Indian provider's [USD marketing card](https://hostiware.com/linux-dedicated-servers) describes an exclusive physical Dedicated 05, 9454P 48 cores, 256 GB DDR5 ECC registered, 4 TB NVMe, 1 Gbps and 32 TB traffic, for $270 monthly or $3,240 annually. The INR card for the same name explicitly has two older 7282 CPUs. The [USD product's actual order route](https://clientarea.hostiware.com/store/dedicated-server/dedicated-05?currency=1&billingcycle=monthly) and its annual equivalent request a customer domain. More importantly, the [live billing catalog with USD selected](https://clientarea.hostiware.com/store/dedicated-server?currency=1) assigns the same Dedicated 05 product to one or two Xeon E5-2630 v4 processors, 256 GB and **4,000 GB HDD**, at **$228.99/month**. That catalog links back to the same product route. Neither price provides verified purchasing evidence for this 9454P configuration. The generic sale banner asks customers to chat for up to 50% off, without a matching coupon, term or expiry.
