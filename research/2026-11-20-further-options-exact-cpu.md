## 99RDP

The [launch page](https://99rdp.com/new-plan-launch/) advertises the two machines at $309 and $359. A generic introductory paragraph describes 40 cores, while both named launch-plan cards, the [live billing catalogue](https://portal.99rdp.com/store/new-plan-launch) and both selected carts explicitly identify the 48-core/96-thread EPYC 9454P. The actual monthly calculator charge is $449.99 for either chassis. The shop displays recurring term discounts of 2%, 4% and 6% at three, six and twelve months respectively. The independently selected term totals and due-today amounts match. No extra setup or tax line was charged by the anonymous calculator. The [terms](https://99rdp.com/terms-of-service/) specify prices before applicable tax and cover credit/debit card, net banking, PayPal and cheque. Cancellation may take effect at the end of the prepaid term. None of those checks confirmed physical allocation, SKU-specific location or payment eligibility by country.
