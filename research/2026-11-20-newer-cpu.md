## Additional configurations with material conditions

**DedicatedNodes, Rotterdam, Netherlands.** [7975WX product](https://www.dedicatednodes.io/bare-metal-server/amd-ryzen-threadripper-pro-7975wx/), [7975WX cart](https://portal.dedicatednodes.io/index.php?rp=/store/bare-metal-servers/amd-ryzen-threadripper-pro-7975wx), [9975WX cart](https://portal.dedicatednodes.io/index.php?rp=/store/bare-metal-servers/amd-ryzen-threadripper-pro-9975wx), [9475F cart](https://portal.dedicatednodes.io/index.php?rp=/store/bare-metal-servers/amd-epyc-9475f), [terms](https://www.dedicatednodes.io/terms-and-conditions/). Fresh cookie-preserving sessions reached all three full hardware configurators. The configurator's public calculation returned the complete quote with Rotterdam, 256 GB, 1 × 2 TB Samsung 9100 Pro NVMe, 10 Gbps and a free Debian installation. Product catalogue gives 335 TB outbound per month. One SSD provides no disk redundancy. Each quote itemized zero setup. One-month contract auto-renews monthly. Longer billing periods are prepaid. The provider separately advertises two to three weeks for custom configurations, not reserved stock.

| EPYC 9475F | €728 | €8,037.12 | A stronger 48-core server CPU; published all-customer gross quote exceeds the specified net ceiling. Some VAT-registered EU businesses qualify for lower terms. |
| Threadripper PRO 9975WX | €728 | €8,037.12 | 32-core CPU; aggregate score ahead. Same jurisdiction-dependent price. |

[Article 6](https://www.dedicatednodes.io/terms-and-conditions/) states that all website list prices include Dutch VAT. For a VAT-registered business in a different EU member state the provider describes the amount as the “list price divided by one plus the VAT rate of its own member state.” All remaining buyers, including non-EU customers, pay the full list amount according to the same article. Customer-specific reverse-charge billing is not a universal ex-tax quote. No customer's location or VAT registration was supplied. A buyer with a qualifying 19% rate would pay €611.76 per month or €6,753.88 per prepaid year, about €562.82 per month effective, for the 9475F/9975WX configuration. The publicly displayed €728 or €8,037.12 is the generally payable amount for other customers under the stated terms. The charge shown by the live configurator supersedes inconsistent advertised starting prices: e.g. the 7975WX product advertises €615/384 GB whereas its configurator actually offers 256 GB and the €578 complete total above.

[PassMark's exact 7975WX comparison](https://www.cpubenchmark.net/compare/5729vs5596/AMD-Ryzen-Threadripper-PRO-7975WX-vs-AMD-EPYC-9454P) shows 95,566 vs 94,689 aggregate (+0.9%) and 3,989 vs 2,988 single-thread (+33.5%). Actual submitted-system subtests show substantially lower throughput in core workloads: [7975WX](https://www.cpubenchmark.net/cpu.php?cpu=AMD+Ryzen+Threadripper+PRO+7975WX&id=5729) vs [9454P](https://www.cpubenchmark.net/cpu.php?cpu=AMD+EPYC+9454P&id=5596) integer math 406,264 vs 450,096 MOps/s, string sorting 178,234 vs 233,062 thousand strings/s and data compression 1,496,755 vs 1,668,348 KB/s. The very narrow overall margin masks this pattern, so the 7975WX is a workload-dependent option. [9975WX vs 9454P](https://www.cpubenchmark.net/compare/5596vs6799/AMD-EPYC-9454P-vs-AMD-Ryzen-Threadripper-PRO-9975WX) shows 105,309 vs 94,689 aggregate, and 4,420 vs 2,988 single-thread. In single-socket Dell systems the 9475F scored [688 integer rate](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2025q3/cpu2017-20250616-48768.html) vs [520 on the 9454P](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2023q2/cpu2017-20230421-35918.pdf) and [19.9 integer speed](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2025q4/cpu2017-20251117-50442.html) vs [14.2](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2023q2/cpu2017-20230421-35938.html). Integer speed is a single-copy benchmark, not a guaranteed single-thread measurement. The systems and software differ.
