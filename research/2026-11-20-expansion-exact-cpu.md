## HOSTING.WORKS, Ukraine — four exact EPYC 9454P offers

The Ukrainian provider [HOSTING.WORKS](https://hosting.works/dedicated) publishes four identifiable whole-server configurations with a single 48-core EPYC 9454P (96 threads with SMT), DDR5 ECC, full root access, 1 Gbit/s and unlimited traffic. One dedicated IPv4 and an IPv6 /64 are included. The service details explicitly specify these network allowances. Each product page embeds the site's product and pricing data in the server-rendered Angular app state, with the same plan ID and separate EUR, UAH and UAH_JURO monthly and setup fields. Each product has a distinct purchase button that opens the provider's own billing app. No buyer-specific tax, assigned inventory, delivery time or minimum contract was established. The monthly billing period in the data is one month. No annual discount is assumed.

## ServerFee, Iran — low IRR quote, different EUR catalogue

The provider's [Iranian-rial catalogue](https://my.serverfee.com/store/dedicated-server-euro) has the [AX162-R, PID 160](https://my.serverfee.com/cart.php?a=add&pid=160) and [AX162-S, PID 159](https://my.serverfee.com/cart.php?a=add&pid=159). Independent anonymous carts showed the same base 226,311,500 **IRR**/month plus 90,715,500 IRR setup, monthly billing only. The catalogue states 228,221,500 IRR/month for both. An optional primary IPv4 at 1,910,000 IRR explains the arithmetic difference; its box in the configurator was unchecked. The base is 22,631,150 Iranian toman, setup 9,071,550 toman. The generic empty cart's default tax estimate shows 10% VAT, buyer-specific invoices unverified.

R is 256 GB registered DDR5 ECC, 2 × 1.92 TB Gen4 NVMe, S is 128 GB registered DDR5 ECC, 2 × 3.84 TB Gen4 NVMe. Both specify the actual 9454P, Helsinki HEL1 and unlimited 1 Gbit/s. Physical stock and eligibility for non-Iranian customers are unverified. Importantly, choosing “EURO” in the live storefront produced [a different public catalogue](https://my.serverfee.com/store/dedicated-server-euro?currency=9), without the R/S SKUs, and no exact euro checkout. The direct rial prices are meaningful for people who can use this billing channel, but should not be mapped to USD from a third-party currency rate or an assumed card eligibility. A declared EUR figure is not supported. A sale was not submitted.
