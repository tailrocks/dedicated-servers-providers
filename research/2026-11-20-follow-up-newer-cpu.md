## New qualifying seller, PowerVPS.net

This is PowerVPS.net. PowerDedi is a different brand. Both the [English storefront](https://powervps.net/) and the [operator's dated hardware reference](https://kb.powervps.net/?p=713) returned HTTP 200 in independent anonymous direct requests. The configurations appear under dedicated servers, with KVM VPS offerings in another section. The hardware reference names physical core counts and distinguishes a processor core from a VPS vCPU. Both CPUs are 48 cores / 96 threads. Direct CSS/HTML inspection of both storefronts confirms the monthly prices in their dedicated-server cards. The [Polish storefront](https://powervps.net/pl/) corroborates them.

| CPU | Model-specific location | Installed configuration | Native monthly | 12 months of rent before any unknown fees |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| EPYC 9454P | Germany or Finland, exact country of the unit unconfirmed | 256 GB DDR5 ECC, 2 × 2 TB NVMe, 1 Gbps port | USD 320 | USD 3,840 |
| EPYC 9455, non-P Turin | France, city unspecified | 128 GB RAM, 2 × 2 TB NVMe, 1 Gbps port | USD 405 | USD 4,860 |

Both configurations provide 4 TB nominal raw or 2 TB if mirrored. The provider advertises unlimited traffic on its homepage, and the technical reference describes a 1 Gbps port without guaranteeing that rate to each user. The website describes the 256 GB on the 9454P as DDR5 ECC. The 9455 card gives capacity without a DIMM type, so leave it as 128 GB RAM. Model-specific evidence for the 9455 is in the repository methodology. [Single-socket Lenovo integer rate, 627](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2025q2/cpu2017-20250324-47137.html) versus [525 on the reference](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2023q1/cpu2017-20230227-34459.html), plus [Dell single-copy integer speed, 18.6](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2026q1/cpu2017-20260109-50919.html) versus [14.2](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2023q2/cpu2017-20230421-35938.html). These are published system measurements. SPECspeed is a single-copy workload.

**Listed, account-gated.** Every Order button opens the same [registration page](https://dash.powervps.net/billmgr?func=register). No provider-specific active unit count, guaranteed allocation, selected anonymous invoice, IP allocation, hardware setup fee or term discount was confirmed. Free Windows installation and setup assistance are advertised independently of any hardware setup fee. The [linked legal PDF](https://powervps.net/legal/terms.pdf), section 4.1, states applicable statutory VAT is additional, but prints the future revision date “07.03.2027” and gives no complete contracting company name in its identify-the-operator paragraph. Treat applicable tax and contract details as unconfirmed for this research date. Monthly rates without a verified discounted prepaid term. The hardware reference explicitly leaves the 9454P unit's country to provider confirmation. No additional discount or more precise location is assumed.
