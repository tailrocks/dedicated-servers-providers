## Faciotech — new matching quoted configuration

The [dedicated-server configurator](https://faciotech.com/dedicated-servers.php#configurator) publishes Dedicated Enterprise Metal with **one EPYC 9454P, 48 cores / 96 threads, 256 GB DDR5 ECC, two 1.92 TB NVMe SSDs in RAID 1**, IPv4/IPv6, DDoS protection and a KVM console. The default supported dedicated region is Germany. North America is on request; Africa is described as launching soon. Neither is established as an allocatable alternative for this SKU.

The page's public price data and calculation code specify:

| Option | Recurring USD | Setup | First 12 months |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Unmanaged, full root access | 389/month | 0 | 4,668 |
| Managed, including DirectAdmin and monitoring | 549/month | 0 | 6,588 |

The same monthly rate applies to annual billing, so annual rent is USD 4,668 or USD 6,588 respectively, with no rent discount. The two-year option requests an engineer's quote; no two-year recurring amount is established. The page explicitly identifies USD and excludes tax. Optional licenses, extra IPv4 and monitoring are not mandatory for the unmanaged base. The page says the renewal price equals the displayed price, subject to the accepted configuration.

An older indexed version still describes USD 79–99 setup on monthly terms and 1 Gbps unmetered. Fresh primary-page retrieval instead shows zero setup in the term selector, FAQ, no-JavaScript summary and calculation code, while omitting a SKU-specific bandwidth commitment. This record follows the current page and leaves network details unconfirmed; the older search snippet is not substituted for the current configuration.

The configured action opens a contact/reservation enquiry carrying the settings. It does not create a paid server order, and the provider explicitly requires an engineer to validate the configuration, availability and term price before billing. Thus the README labels this **CONFIGURATOR QUOTE**, not confirmed inventory or an automatically provisionable cart. The SKU-specific port speed, traffic quota, exact German city and delivery date were not published in the inspected configuration. The two drives total 3.84 TB raw and 1.92 TB mirrored.

The [terms](https://faciotech.com/terms-of-service.php) say the contracting business is identified in the accepted order or agreement and distinguish its Canadian and Ghanaian businesses. Currency and payment processor do not identify the legal seller by themselves. Applicable taxes must be disclosed before payment. No assumption about the customer's tax jurisdiction or eligibility is made.

## Availability and interpretation

An explicit sold-out result in a seller's own order feed is stronger evidence than its low marketing price. Separately queried upstream stock can inform the risk without proving that every reseller lacks preallocated inventory. The detailed logs state which kind of evidence was observed.

The 2 TB requirement continues to mean raw installed SSD capacity. Two 1.92 TB drives qualify as 3.84 TB raw but give only 1.92 TB mirrored. Currency conversion is a comparison calculation; it must not be represented as an available USD billing route when the seller only invoices another currency.

## Additional dated exchange rates

The earlier EUR/USD and EUR/BRL reference observations remain unchanged. The new currencies use the same **19 November 2026** ECB observation date, the latest published business-day rates at the time of research:

- [GBP per EUR](https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.GBP.EUR.SP00.A?startPeriod=2026-11-19&endPeriod=2026-11-19&format=csvdata): **0.83581**. Convert as `GBP / 0.83581 * 1.0992`.
- [MYR per EUR](https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.MYR.EUR.SP00.A?startPeriod=2026-11-19&endPeriod=2026-11-19&format=csvdata): **4.5205**. Convert as `MYR / 4.5205 * 1.0992`.

Compute prepaid equivalents and first-year totals from the quoted native payment amount, before final rounding. The method does not assume that a GBP- or MYR-priced seller will accept USD at the comparison value.
