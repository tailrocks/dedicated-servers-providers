# Additional global dedicated server offers, 19 November 2026

Evidence record from the 2026-11-19 research pass. The [README](../README.md) is the canonical comparison; [the consolidated history](2026-11-19.md#continuation-of-research) records later corrections across parallel checks.

The benchmark reference is one AMD EPYC 9454P. Capacity here means decimal raw SSD capacity before RAID. The reference regular Hetzner AX162-1 has 128 GB and 2 × 3.84 TB. Listings with this exact CPU need no performance estimate. Search and review used public information. No orders, account creation, or vendor contacts.

## Exclusions and additional leads

- [UHost AX162-S](https://uhost.online/en/dedicated/20) is another exact-CPU published configurator: USD 363/mo Helsinki or USD 367/mo Falkenstein, USD 120 setup; 128 GB DDR5 ECC, 2 × 3.84 TB Gen4 NVMe, software RAID1 (3.84 TB nominal mirrored capacity). The [dedicated catalog](https://uhost.online/en/dedicated) repeats the R and S pricing and says usually available in minutes for each. It also advertises a new-customer discount of 50% for a new one-month order or 25% when paying for three months, with an email form to get details. Hardware-specific coupon, discount base, renewal and end date were not shown. Use regular recurring prices; IPv4 is called paid in the catalog but shows USD 0 in the configurator.
- [ACECA physical workstation rentals](https://aceca.com/vfx-workstations/) surfaced an advertised USD 570 7975WX base. The provider specifies 64 GB base and USD 200/month per added 64 GB. At USD 770 for qualifying RAM it misses the USD 722.10 ceiling before any hosting costs. [Varidata](https://www.varidata.com/zh-tw/server/hk/cn2/) lists dual 9554 in Hong Kong at HKD 9,409/mo, well over the equivalent ceiling; the cheaper single 7713/7C13 is below performance floor and has 2 × 960 GB. [HostMeNow](https://hostmenow.org/zh-TW/backstage/index.php/store/dedicated-plans) 9455 starts GBP 1,881.98/mo. [Potenting Singapore](https://potenting.com/zh/dedicated-servers/singapore/) examples include a 9455 at USD 1,453.20 plus USD 963.02 setup. No qualifying alternative in these results.

- [NetVO](https://netvo.io/store/dedicated-servers) lists the 9455, 128 GB, 2 × 1.92 TB France at USD 434.99 plus USD 435 setup, and the Europe researcher recovered [the selected configurator](https://netvo.io/cart.php?a=add&pid=66) with an anonymous cookie-preserving session. The generic redirect during web indexing had dropped the selection. See the Europe research for full product terms. Its genuine exact 9454P listing is USD 888.99 plus USD 440, above baseline. OVH RISE-XL has [its own official configurator](https://www.kimsufi.com/en/rise/rise-xl/?dc=rbx), USD 354 + USD 354, 128 GB, 2 × 1.92 TB. Region-specific inventory requires confirmation; France and Canada may be unavailable. Avoid resale site as independent hardware.
- [Cherry Servers 9474F](https://www.cherryservers.com/pricing/dedicated-servers/amd-epyc-9474f?billing=3), 384 GB and 2 × 1 TB NVMe, showed USD 1,051.83 monthly or USD 894.06 effective with annual prepayment, above baseline even on term. [AMD CPU comparison by specification](https://www.amd.com/content/dam/amd/en/documents/epyc-technical-docs/data-sheets/221700653-C_en_AMD-EPYC-9004-Series-Processors---Data-Sheet_pdf.pdf).
- [FiberState](https://www.fiberstate.com/dedicated-servers) lists Salt Lake City 9654 at USD 995.95 monthly, 128 GB/2 TB NVMe, and 7960X at USD 399.95 with 256 GB/2 TB; first over price, second below throughput floor. [OpenMetal](https://openmetal.io/bare-metal-pricing/) Xeon 6 machines return four-figure monthly prices. [Shine Servers](https://one.shineservers.com/c/bare-metal-dedicated-servers) qualifying midrange Scale models in Mumbai/Singapore are above the US-dollar ceiling.
- [Heymman Kansas City store](https://support.heymman.com/cart.php) advertises Dell C6525 with dual 7713, 512 GB ECC, 2 TB Samsung 990 Pro, 100 TB at 10 Gbps at USD 219/mo. Older [promo-linked order](https://support.heymman.com/cart.php?a=add&pid=467) says out of stock. Aggregate-only candidate even if a different current order exists; [dual 7713 benchmark](https://www.cpubenchmark.net/cpu.php?cpu=AMD+EPYC+7713&cpuCount=2&id=4206) is below [the 9454P](https://www.cpubenchmark.net/cpu.php?id=5596) in single-thread speed. Avoid giving an unscoped discount to unrelated configurations.

- [Nodestop](https://www.nodestop.io/store/amd-epyc-dedicated) quotes USD 6,699.90 semi-annually for 9474F, 256 GB or higher, two 2 TB or larger NVMe plus boot disk, in US New York or Ashburn.
