**Actual dual-socket CPU evidence.** [AMD specifications](https://www.amd.com/en/products/processors/server/epyc/4th-generation-9004-and-8004-series/amd-epyc-9474f.html) give 48 cores and 96 threads for each 9474F. Lenovo's SR675 V3 reports **96 physical cores across two chips**, 2 threads/core, 1,536 GB memory. Its [SPEC integer throughput](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2023q4/cpu2017-20231120-39932.html) is 1,100 versus [520 on the reference Dell 9454P](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2023q2/cpu2017-20230421-35918.pdf), about +112%. SPECspeed is a single-copy test. Platform, RAM population and compiler differ, so these are model-performance comparisons, not measurements of an offered server.

**Watchlist only.** The [seller's description](https://bitcloudvps.com/about/) identifies Harborline Node Ltd. in Nevis, and its [terms](https://bitcloudvps.com/terms/) describe prepaid cryptocurrency settlement; the [refund description](https://bitcloudvps.com/refund/) says unused prepaid time is normally credited to the account. Neither these displays nor the [VPS-family benchmark page](https://bitcloudvps.com/benchmarks/) establish physical stock or independent fulfillment of Dedicated Ultra in any of those locations.
