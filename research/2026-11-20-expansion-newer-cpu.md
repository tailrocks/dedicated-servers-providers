The [provider announcement](https://dediroom.com/announcements/4/NEW-High-Performance-Dedicated-Servers--Now-Available.html) advertised a Naaldwijk, Netherlands, non-virtualized HPE ProLiant DL320 Gen12 machine at EUR 600/month. Formula €71 chassis + €279 CPU + €102.40 256 GB DDR5 registered ECC + €47.60 for 2 × 1.92 TB NVMe + €50 for the 10 Gbps/100 TB network, free Ubuntu, Netherlands DC2, no extras. No separate setup line shown. Monthly only; IP count not established. The [current High-End category](https://dediroom.com/store/high-end-dedicated-servers) does not list this 6761P even though its specific announcement link and exact cart still work. Its stated two-hour delivery is not proof of allocation. The supplier's [terms §18](https://dediroom.com/TermsAndConditions.php) exclude applicable taxes. The 6741P in the current category is a different, smaller system with 1.92 TB RAW and does not meet the disk floor. [CloudHosting](https://www.cloudhosting.lv/10gbps-dedicated-servers) lists a 6761P well over budget (EUR 1,890 base/month; EUR 1,606.50 on its displayed 15% term).

**CPU evidence and caveat.** The [Intel Xeon 6761P](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/products/sku/241842/intel-xeon-6761p-processor-336m-cache-2-50-ghz/specifications.html) is a 64-core/128-thread part. [PassMark](https://www.cpubenchmark.net/cpu.php?cpu=Intel+Xeon+6761P&id=7286) reports 116,690 CPU Mark and 3,251 single thread, +23.2% and +8.8% compared with the [9454P](https://www.cpubenchmark.net/cpu.php?cpu=AMD+EPYC+9454P&id=5596) at 94,689 and 2,988; the Intel result has **only one submission** and is marked high margin of error. An HPE DL320 Gen12 with this actual CPU scores [677 SPEC integer throughput](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2025q2/cpu2017-20250323-47074.html) vs [520 on the Dell 9454P](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2023q2/cpu2017-20230421-35918.pdf), while that HPE's [single-copy integer speed was 14.1](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2025q2/cpu2017-20250323-47075.html) vs the 9454P's [14.2](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2023q2/cpu2017-20230421-35938.html). A [Dell Xeon 6761P](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2025q2/cpu2017-20250418-47551.pdf) reached 14.2; [Supermicro's 6761P](https://www.spec.org/cpu2017/results/res2025q2/cpu2017-20250407-47459.html) reached 13.1. SPECspeed is single-copy, not guaranteed single-thread. These vary by system and software and are not tests of the rental. For the strict 'no lower performance' interpretation, keep as a workload-dependent candidate; disclose that it is clearly above on throughput and approximately level on HPE SPECspeed with modest counterevidence. Do not describe the excluded Intel quote as a hardware/pricing failure.
