## Quicservers

The Indian provider's [Finland](https://quicservers.com/dedicated-servers/dedicated-servers-finland/) and [Germany](https://quicservers.com/dedicated-servers/dedicated-servers-germany/) pages both identify a High Performance whole physical server with EPYC 9454P, 256 GB RAM and two 1.92 TB NVMe drives at **Rs. 32,000/month**. Both pages define the dedicated product as an entire physical server for one customer, promise free initial setup, and advertise root access, IPMI, a RAID controller and a choice of 100/1000 Mbps port speeds across the range. Neither identifies the selected machine's port speed, actual RAID level, traffic allowance, included IPs, RAM generation, city or physical stock. The hardware yields 3.84 TB raw or 1.92 TB if mirrored. The [Indian contact page](https://quicservers.com/contact-us/) and the site's [explicit INR labels](https://quicservers.com/colocation/) establish the rupee pricing convention. No alternate USD tariff or payment gateway was recovered.

The row's Buy Now text has no hyperlink to a product or cart. The page offers a generic callback form. The [terms](https://quicservers.com/terms-of-service/) describe the topics a service policy typically covers but do not specify the dedicated seller's minimum term, payment eligibility, tax rate or advance commitment. The hardware-specific pages quote monthly prices. Terms stated for other services are not assumed. At the published price, INR 32,000 per month with free setup gives INR 384,000 over twelve months before unresolved tax. The [19 November ECB INR observation](https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.INR.EUR.SP00.A?startPeriod=2026-11-19&endPeriod=2026-11-19&format=csvdata) returned INR 102.55 per EUR; with the repository's USD 1.0992 per EUR, the comparisons are **USD 343.00/month and USD 4,115.97/year**. The latter is illustrative monthly rent extended over twelve months, not an annual prepaid offer. Use one row for both countries. The separate US and Mumbai catalogs checked did not identify this configuration.

## TRAIDNT GROUP

The Saudi company's [private-server listing](https://traidnt.com/ar/services/private-servers) advertises T-SERVER 4 at SAR 1,500 monthly. Its [dedicated billing category](https://manage.traidnt.com/store/dedicated-servers) exposes T-SERVER 4, PID 91, and SAUDI S5, PID 170, with identical specs and prices. Direct cookie-preserving GETs on the [T-SERVER 4 product](https://manage.traidnt.com/store/dedicated-servers/t-server4) and [SAUDI S5 product](https://manage.traidnt.com/store/dedicated-servers/saudi-s5) opened their actual configuration forms. Cookie-free redirects can lead to the shared-hosting category. Each dedicated form names the 48-core 9454P, 256 GB DDR5 ECC registered memory, two 1.92 TB Gen4 datacenter NVMe SSDs in software RAID 1, and unlimited traffic. Port rate was not shown. The physical datacenter for neither EPYC SKU was established. The site's separately advertised [Riyadh service](https://traidnt.com/ar/services/saudi-servers) specifies Arm VPS and does not locate these x86 machines. Consolidate the otherwise identical SKUs. The name SAUDI S5 alone does not establish a separate Saudi deployment.

The anonymous option-price calculators for **both** selected products returned SAR 1,500/month + SAR 2,000 once, **SAR 3,500 due initially**. The selected options were Ubuntu 24.04 LTS, one IP and no cPanel, each at SAR 0 additional charge. Only the monthly term was displayed. The [seller's terms](https://traidnt.com/en/terms) require payment upon ordering and restrict refunds after server preparation and handover. No specific stock count, contractual minimum beyond the selected month, final buyer tax, currency settlement for international cards or guaranteed provisioning date was established. The selected total contained no separate tax line. At a flat rate for twelve months, including setup, the subtotal is **SAR 20,000**. The [Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye bulletin of 19 November 2026](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/kurlar/202611/19112026.xml), bulletin 2026/218, states SAR CrossRateUSD = 3.7512, i.e. USD 1 = SAR 3.7512. Using the official dated cross, rent is **USD 399.87**, setup USD 533.16, initial USD 933.03 and 12 months plus setup USD 5,331.63. Billing stays in SAR. The [Saudi Central Bank's published 3.75 policy peg](https://www.sama.gov.sa/en-US/MediaCenter/News/Pages/news-557.aspx) is a separate measure.

## Conflicting low prices and unavailable offers

- **Mostdeef, Egypt.** Its [current unmanaged dedicated sales page](https://mostdeef.com/our_services/unmanged-dedicated-servers/) and [homepage](https://mostdeef.com/) both send the Premium card to the [same current billing product](https://clients.mostdeef.com/index.php?rp=/store/unmanaged-dedicated-servers/premium-dedicated-server). Both sales cards explicitly promise **Ryzen 9 7950X3D / 128 GB**, at USD 395.99/month, USD 2,304.66 per six months or USD 4,514.29 per year. The [monthly PID 31 configurator](https://clients.mostdeef.com/cart.php?a=add&pid=31&currency=1&billingcycle=monthly&language=english) and billing store instead say **EPYC 9454P / 256 GB DDR5 ECC RDIMM / 2 × 1.92 TB NVMe / unlimited bandwidth**. The selected monthly calculator confirmed USD 395.99 recurring and due, explicit zero setup, USD 4,751.88 in twelve monthly payments before unknown tax. The [six-month](https://clients.mostdeef.com/cart.php?a=add&pid=31&currency=1&billingcycle=semiannually&language=english) and [annual](https://clients.mostdeef.com/cart.php?a=add&pid=31&currency=1&billingcycle=annually&language=english) selectors both imply USD 0.33/month; the calculators actually return **USD 2 per six-month term** and **USD 4 per annual term**, each with zero setup. Those amounts conflict with the seller's own four-figure prepayment prices. An older [Windows category](https://mostdeef.com/clients/index.php?currency=3&rp=/store/dedicatedwindows) was indexed at SAR 937.50/month with the EPYC. Fresh direct requests follow the change to the unmanaged category; the older Windows name, implied USD 250, two discount calculations and present generic Premium hardware cannot support a strict replacement. Exact currency and cart totals are known, but fulfillment hardware/term is unresolved. Datacenter, tax, physical stock and payment-processor settlement were unconfirmed.
- **Sahaba Host.** The live [German EPYC model card](https://sahaba-host.com/server.php) advertises an entire root server with 9454P, 256 GB DDR5 ECC RDIMM, two 1.92 TB Gen4 NVMe in RAID 1 and unmetered 1 Gbps at a displayed **$410/month**. The [older contact page](https://www.tele-serv.com/contact.php) provides only a generic inquiry route, and the [hosting overview](https://tele-serv.com/hosting.php) describes management as included.
