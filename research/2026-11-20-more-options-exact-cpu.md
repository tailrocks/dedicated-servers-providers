The [DSA-5 plan](https://purenext.de/dedicated-server/) specifies 128 GB registered DDR5 ECC, 2 × 3.84 TB Gen4 NVMe and an unmetered 1 Gbit/s port. It costs **€265.23/month (~$291.54) plus €513.99 setup**. The listed amounts imply €779.22 initially and €3,696.75 in year one. The seller identifies Hetzner Falkenstein, Germany as its [hosting datacenter](https://purenext.de/ueber-uns/rechenzentrum). The product page describes preset hardware and explains that final provisioning, availability and a binding quote are arranged by inquiry. Quote only, no confirmed physical stock, exact unit location or included IP allocation.

The [terms](https://purenext.de/agb) specify a **one-month minimum and advance payment**, with available payment methods included on the invoice. Cancel by email through the end of a month for effect on the first of the following month. The seller applies the German small-business rule and does not charge VAT. It identifies dedicated servers as a resold service, not separately controlled inventory. Consumer and business sales are covered. Specific foreign payment methods remain unverified. The [imprint](https://purenext.de/impressum) names sole proprietor Timo Goeres and German VAT ID DE449530580.

**Sibling DSA-4.** €265.23 monthly +€669.88, 256 GB registered DDR5 ECC, 2 × 1.92 TB marked only “Gen 4.” NVMe is mentioned among optional upgrades, but the included drives omit “NVMe” and “SSD” where DSA-5 explicitly labels both. Retain as a conditional lead. The DSA-5 is the recommended row.
