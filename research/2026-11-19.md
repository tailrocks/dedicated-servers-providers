# Dedicated-server research — 2026-11-19

This log preserves the earlier conversation's findings and the continuing research. The current comparison is the table in [README.md](../README.md); the qualification and currency rules are in [METHODOLOGY.md](../METHODOLOGY.md). Observations below are evidence records, not assurances that a vendor can deliver a server.

The strict floor is a physical dedicated server, EPYC 9454P-class CPU performance, at least 128 GB RAM and at least 2 TB raw SSD capacity. The earlier broad budget search also considered weaker hardware; those findings remain here as exclusions instead of being passed off as equal-performance alternatives.

### Performance, stock and cost corrections

1. **9355P is conditional.** A slightly higher PassMark aggregate score did not survive a broader workload check: the Lenovo SR635 V3 SPEC integer-rate result was 478 versus 525 for the 9454P, while floating-point was higher. The main table therefore excludes the 9355P as an unconditional match. Full CPU sources and limitations are in [METHODOLOGY.md](../METHODOLOGY.md#cross-checks-beyond-passmark).
2. **Non-P 9455 has direct benchmark evidence.** Its Lenovo integer-rate result of 627 exceeds 525 for the 9454P; Dell has 624 versus 520. Do not silently assign the 9455P or embedded model's PassMark result to it.
3. **ENTRONYX is a current advertised lead with history to disclose.** The earlier indexed demonstration notice was removed from current B2B pages. Current pricing logic fixes setup at €299.99 in both Helsinki and Germany, and waives it for prepayment. Helsinki totals are €3,683.87 for monthly billing in year one, €3,214.68 annual prepayment or €6,226.32 for 24 months.
