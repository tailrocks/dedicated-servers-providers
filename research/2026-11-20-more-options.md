## Arxan: lowest configured net quote, with a visible tax and payment distinction

The [physical-server catalogue](https://hosting.arxan.app/store/dedicated-server) labels the product AMD EPYC 9454P, 48 cores, 256 GB DDR5 ECC, two 1.92 TB datacenter Gen4 NVMe SSDs in software RAID 1, one IPv4 and a 1 Gbps port. Its generic installation estimate is 3–5 days; it does not publish an allocated unit or a CPU-specific datacenter. Shared hosting on another part of the site is labelled Singapore, which does **not** establish this dedicated server's location.

A cookie-less request to the branded product initially returned an unrelated shared-hosting category. A fresh ordinary anonymous session at the published numeric product ID 45 reached the matching configuration. A second independent check also resolved the [branded product route](https://hosting.arxan.app/store/dedicated-server/amd-epyc-9454p) to the same hardware. Only the monthly billing period was offered. ## Reference, normalization and why full payments matter

The [Hetzner configurator](https://www.hetzner.com/dedicated-rootserver/ax162/configurator/) and freshly retrieved [official live price feed](https://www.hetzner.com/_resources/app/data/app/live_data_prices.json), `ROBOT_1724`, still show **€612.30/month + €304 setup** for NBG1, FSN1 and HEL1. That is **€7,651.60 for twelve rents plus setup** before VAT/IPv4. The separate USD tariff remains $722.10/month + $359 setup. At the dated EUR/USD comparison below, the EUR tariff is ~$673.04/month and ~$8,410.64 first year.

- [ECB 19 November EUR/USD](https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.USD.EUR.SP00.A?startPeriod=2026-11-19&endPeriod=2026-11-19&format=csvdata): EUR 1 = USD 1.0992.
- [ECB 19 November EUR/IDR](https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.IDR.EUR.SP00.A?startPeriod=2026-11-19&endPeriod=2026-11-19&format=csvdata): EUR 1 = IDR 20,073.1. Convert IDR through EUR, retaining full precision until the final displayed amount.
- [NBP 19 November UAH](https://api.nbp.pl/api/exchangerates/rates/a/uah/2026-11-19/?format=json) and [USD](https://api.nbp.pl/api/exchangerates/rates/a/usd/2026-11-19/?format=json): table 225/A/NBP/2026, one UAH = PLN 0.0891 and one USD = PLN 4.0176. Convert actual UAH term payments as `UAH × 0.0891 ÷ 4.0176`, then divide by term length. These official observations were independently retrieved during integration.

Cloud VPS Hosts' actual annual rent is €3,424, not twelve times the rounded €285.33 display. Hosting House removes setup from three selected annual SKUs but retains €158 on the German 128 GB model. Fit Servers' $364 monthly offer carries $1,496.35 setup. REDS CLOUD's marketing USD reference differs from its selected UAH quote converted at the official comparison rate. These distinctions materially change rankings and upfront cash requirements.

DDS advertises **DEDI2026** for 10% off month one. The code was recognized as a one-time promotion on an empty basket, but a discounted total on the populated exact-server basket was not obtained without providing required provisioning fields. The main price remains €299.99 recurring. €269.99 month one and €3,569.88 first year are only advertised-code calculations, pending a valid selected checkout; they are not reported as observed charges.
