## European and international choices

**A1 capacity** means at least 128 GB in one host and two drives giving at least 3.84 TB nominal mirrored. **A2 capacity** means at least 256 GB in one host and two drives giving at least 1.92 TB nominal mirrored. These profiles can neither be substituted for each other nor satisfied by adding the RAM of separate machines. A stated pair of drives provides the *potential* for RAID 1 where array configuration is unspecified.

The same-model EU rentals are the most direct way to test the purchase economics without assuming an architectural speedup. The 16-core plans address serial tasks or independent worker queues. The 64-core Threadripper and newer EPYC options address wider parallel stages. Storage capacity, network allowance, payment route, supplier terms and usable memory per job narrow the comparison in each case. Public listings, price feeds and anonymous selection screens establish quote evidence only.
