# Buying a Linux CI host in Malaysia

**Public listings checked 20 November 2026, Asia/Saigon. All prices here are Malaysian ringgit.** The local 128 GB Intel desktop with a two-drive 2 TB mirror totals **RM19,153**, or **RM23,357 with a 4 TB mirror**. A 256 GB version using four exact Kingston non-ECC UDIMMs costs **RM32,211**. The local 9950X3D desktop costs **RM20,853**, or **RM41,706** for two workers. Malaysia has a credible desktop DDR5 purchase path. A complete locally priced 5995WX workstation with equivalent supply evidence was unavailable. ## Fit, cooling and support

Both micro-ATX boards fit the Cooler Master Elite 301. Its 163.5 mm cooler limit clears the 160 mm dual-tower Phantom Spirit 120 Digital EVO by **3.5 mm** at nominal cooler height. Low-profile standard Kingston DIMMs allow the front fan to retain its stock position. The cooler includes fans and mounting hardware for AM5/LGA1851. Three case fans are included. The MAG A650GL supplies the 24-pin ATX, two EPS connectors and a separate native PCIe 6+2 lead for the MSI board’s PCIE_PWR1. Use the two EPS leads for the CPU inputs. Both CPUs have integrated graphics and both boards have wired networking, 2.5 GbE for ASUS and 5 GbE for MSI. The 2280 NVMe drives fit the built-in M.2 sockets. No separate GPU, NIC, RAID controller, drive adapters or additional fans are required for this bill. The site provides an Ethernet patch lead, switch, mains and initial access to a console. Desktop firmware recovery can require someone on site.
