# Buying a Linux CI host in Singapore

**Public listings checked 20 November 2026, Asia/Saigon. Prices below are Singapore dollars.** A 256 GB, eight-channel 5995WX workstation with two 2 TB NVMe drives totals **S$13,330**. This is the closest single-host purchase candidate to the AX162's CPU. The CPU is a custom order and the cooler has weaker supply evidence. A Core Ultra 7 270K Plus desktop with 128 GB of DDR5 and the same drives costs **S$4,757**. Two independent 128 GB Ryzen 9 9950X3D workers cost **S$10,228**. ### Board, power and cooling

The 64C/128T 280 W 5995WX fits the original sWRX8 board from **BIOS 1003**. Kingston lists the exact UDIMMs for this board. Populate all eight sockets A1 through H1, one module per channel. ASUS supports several memory classes on this board. The bill selects non-ECC UDIMMs throughout. [ASUS processor list](https://www.asus.com/my/supportonly/pro%20ws%20wrx80e-sage%20se%20wifi/helpdesk_cpu/) · [ASUS workstation specifications](https://www.asus.com/sg/motherboards-components/motherboards/workstation/pro-ws-wrx80e-sage-se-wifi/techspec/) · [Memory evidence](ram-comparison.md)

Install both bare M.2 2280 drives under the board's heatsinks in **M.2_1 and M.2_2**. Both connect to the CPU at PCIe 4.0 ×4. Populating M.2_2 makes U.2_1 unavailable. The unused third M.2 socket shares with U.2_2. The case accepts the board's EEB footprint, the 145 mm sWRX8-specific cooler fits its 185 mm clearance, and the cooler allows 53 mm for RAM. ARCTIC supplies MX-6 compound and the mounting kit. Three supplied 140 mm case fans provide chassis airflow. Both M.2 fasteners, BMC video-header cable and case standoffs come from the retail board/case kits. [ASUS manual, board layout, storage and package](https://dlcdnets.asus.com/pub/ASUS/mb/SocketTRX4/Pro_WS_WRX80E-SAGE_SE_WIFI/E19401_Pro_WS_WRX80E-SAGE_SE_WIFI_UM_V2_WEB.pdf) · [ARCTIC dimensions and socket coverage](https://www.arctic.de/en/Freezer-4U-M-Rev.-2/ACFRE00133B) · [Fractal Define 7 XL](https://www.fractal-design.com/products/cases/define/define-7-xl/black-solid/)

Connect the 24-pin ATX cable, both CPU 8-pin EPS cables and the separate board PCIe 8-pin input. The GX-850 retail cable set provides two EPS and sufficient PCIe 6+2 connectors. The two additional 6-pin board connectors, PCIE_PWR_1 and PCIE_PWR_2, are intended for multiple graphics cards. The ASPEED BMC/ASMB9-iKVM supplies console video and remote management. Dual Intel X550-AT2 10 GbE and Linux `ixgbe` supply wired networking. This configuration needs **S$0** of discrete GPU, network card, M.2 adapter, extra fan or specialised cable. [ASUS manual](https://dlcdnets.asus.com/pub/ASUS/mb/SocketTRX4/Pro_WS_WRX80E-SAGE_SE_WIFI/E19401_Pro_WS_WRX80E-SAGE_SE_WIFI_UM_V2_WEB.pdf) · [Seasonic connectors](https://seasonic.com/focus-gx-atx-3/) · [Intel Linux driver](https://docs.kernel.org/networking/device_drivers/ethernet/intel/ixgbe.html)

The ASUS **B860M** board supports the exact 270K Plus. Its integrated graphics supplies local console and Realtek 2.5 GbE supplies Ethernet. Use M.2_1 (CPU PCIe 5.0 ×4) and M.2_2 (chipset PCIe 4.0 ×4). The latter shares the chipset's upstream link with other chipset devices. The MSI **B850M GAMING PLUS WIFI** supports Ryzen 9000, has 5 GbE RTL8126 and routes both M.2 sockets to the 9950X3D, one Gen5 ×4 and one Gen4 ×4. The AMD CPU supplies integrated Radeon graphics. Standard 2280 NM790 drives negotiate Gen4 on either board. A current Linux kernel with the in-tree Realtek `r8169` driver and distribution firmware supports these wired interfaces. Neither desktop includes the workstation's independent BMC, so firmware-level recovery can require a local console.
