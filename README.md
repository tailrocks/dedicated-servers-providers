# Dedicated Servers Providers

Where to buy physical dedicated servers with the best price for **AMD EPYC 9454P-class or higher CPU performance, at least 128GB RAM and at least 2TB raw SSD capacity**. NVMe is preferred. VPS, VDS and dedicated-vCPU products are excluded.

**Last research update: 2026-11-19.** This README is the canonical current comparison. Dated research files retain all earlier findings, failed checks, exclusions and later corrections. A row records a seller's published offer and the evidence reached; it does **not** guarantee allocated hardware or endorse the provider.

## Reference and reading the table

The reference [Hetzner AX162-1](https://www.hetzner.com/dedicated-rootserver/ax162/configurator/) has an EPYC 9454P, 128GB DDR5 ECC and 2x3.84TB NVMe. The official [price list](https://docs.hetzner.com/general/infrastructure-and-availability/price-adjustment/) and [live price data](https://www.hetzner.com/_resources/app/data/app/live_data_prices.json) show **€612.30/month +€304 setup**, before VAT and IPv4. That is **€7,651.60 in year one**. Its separate USD tariff is $722.10/month +$359 setup.

The table keeps the original seven columns and ascending `Price (USD/mo)` sort. EUR conversions use the [ECB rate for 2026-11-19](https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/EXR/D.USD.EUR.SP00.A?startPeriod=2026-11-19&endPeriod=2026-11-19&format=csvdata), **€1 = $1.0992**. `~` means a converted comparison amount, not a provider's USD tariff. **† marks an explicitly tax-inclusive quote** retained without subtracting an unknown VAT rate; other known pre-tax prices are used and unknown tax treatment is stated in Notes. Amounts exclude optional services and licenses. Prepaid conversions use the actual term invoice before rounding.

- **CONFIGURATOR / CART CHECKED:** the matching public hardware and price were reached. Stock, final tax and delivery can still require confirmation. `No setup line shown` is not a binding promise of zero setup.
- **LISTED / QUOTE ONLY:** a provider advertises the matching hardware; a complete selected order or delivery was not verified. Extra identity or historical concerns remain visible.
- **Annual / 24-month / 36-month prepay:** the price is an effective monthly rate. Read the upfront commitment in Notes.
- **Storage is raw capacity.** 2x1.92TB qualifies as 3.84TB raw but yields only 1.92TB when mirrored. Choose 2x2TB or larger if the requirement is 2TB usable with RAID 1.
- **CPU evidence is model-specific.** Exact 9454P rows match the chip. Newer 9455/9455P and Threadripper candidates have benchmark evidence, with platform caveats. EPYC 9355P, 9554P and 9654 have performance tradeoffs and are retained in the research logs rather than presented as unconditional matches.

Several offers are resales of the same datacenter hardware. More sellers do not necessarily mean more physical stock. Read [methodology and CPU evidence](METHODOLOGY.md) before choosing between unlike platforms.

## How to add / update

1. Read [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md) and the latest research log. Verify the whole physical server, exact CPU, installed RAM and SSD capacity from primary sources.
2. Add one row per materially different server or promotion. Keep the original seven columns and sort `Price (USD/mo)` numerically ascending. Do not inflate coverage with identical location copies.
3. Use `CPU / RAM / Storage / Bandwidth` in `Specs`. Give a direct server, configurator or promotion URL; if only a named entry on a range page exists, identify it explicitly.
4. Record native currency, tax basis, setup, contract length, prepayment, stock evidence and performance limitations in `Notes`. Show a lower monthly rate even when useful, but state if first-year total is higher.
5. Update `Last checked` only when that offer is rechecked. Record failed checks and corrections in a dated log, preserving earlier evidence rather than silently deleting it.
6. Keep below-floor CPUs, incomplete disk upgrades, sold-out deals and unresolved demonstrations in the watchlist/log, not among established strict matches. Do not contact sellers, reserve hardware or place orders as part of research.

## Columns

- **Provider:** company / trading name and distinguishable server or contract variant.
- **Location:** advertised datacenter city and country; unknown or selector-only locations are labelled.
- **Specs:** exact CPU / installed RAM / raw drive layout / network port and allowance.
- **Price (USD/mo):** recurring or explicitly labelled prepaid monthly equivalent; EUR conversions marked `~`, retained gross quotes marked `†`. Setup is separate. Unknown taxes are not silently assumed to be zero.
- **URL:** direct offer or configuration, with cookies sometimes needed for public shopping carts.
- **Last checked:** ISO date of the cited price/configuration check.
- **Notes:** evidence level, native quote, tax, setup, term, stock, mirrored capacity and material qualification concerns.
